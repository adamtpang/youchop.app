Good to know

Before you paste a link.

Do I need the extension?

No. Transcript extraction and chapter generation work on this website. The extension is still in development and is not yet published in the Web Store.

Does every video have a transcript?

No. Captions may be unavailable, restricted, or temporarily inaccessible through our transcript provider. YouChop reports failures; a missing result does not necessarily mean YouTube has no captions. Automatic captions can contain mistakes.

Are transcripts and chapters the same thing?

A transcript contains the video's spoken words as captions. Chapters are a shorter list of timestamps and titles. The chapter tool labels basic drafts when AI generation is unavailable so you can review them before use.

Where does my transcript go?

The web tool sends the video ID to our server and transcript providers. Recent extractions are saved locally in this browser and can be cleared from History. Copying or downloading gives you a local copy; YouChop does not post it to YouTube.

Can I extract a whole channel?

The transcript tool includes channel and list modes. Coverage depends on available captions and provider limits; some videos may be missing. Use source links to check the original videos.
